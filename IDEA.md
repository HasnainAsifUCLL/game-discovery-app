# Game Discovery: App Idea

*"Game Discovery" is a working title and the name is subject to change.*

Game Discovery is a personalised game discovery app that helps gamers find new videogames that
match their interests, preferences and play style. Instead of showing a list of popular games,
it lets users discover games they will probably like, even if they would never have searched
for them. On top of that, the app connects gamers with similar interests, so game discovery is
combined with a social side.

Central idea: *Discover games you'll love. Connect with people who love them too.*

There are thousands of games on Steam, PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo. Because of that huge
offer it is hard to decide what to play, discovering new games takes a lot of searching, and
recommendations are often not personal enough. The app makes discovering games easier, more
personal and more fun.

## Target audience

- **Young people** who like playing videogames.
- **Casual gamers** who want to discover new games.
- **Experienced gamers** who are looking for something new.
- **Gamers who want to find people** with similar interests.
- **Adults** who want to find suitable games for their children. The recommendations can take
  age and PEGI ratings into account.

## What am I going to build? (roadmap)

- [x] Discover screen: one game at a time, with Skip and Like buttons
- [x] My Games screen: the games you liked
- [x] Like and Skip work: Like adds the game to My Games, Skip moves on to the next game
- [ ] Swipe on Discover: right to show interest, left to skip a game
- [ ] More information per game: screenshots, gameplay, genre, platform, PEGI rating, playtime
- [ ] My Games collection: favourites, want to play, playing now, finished
- [ ] Recommendations based on genres, tags and likes, with an explanation of why a game is
      recommended
- [ ] People: find gamers with a similar taste, with a compatibility score and common favourite
      games
- [ ] Chats: contact other gamers
- [ ] Profile: favourite games, favourite genres, preferences and settings
- [ ] Choice at setup: discover games for myself or games suitable for my child (PEGI)

## Competitors

- **Steam Discovery Queue:** gives recommendations from a huge store, but is tied to that store
  and is not personal to your play style.
- **Backloggd:** good for logging and rating games, with a social side, but focused on what you
  already played, not on discovering what to play next.
- **HowLongToBeat:** has playtime data per game, but is a reference site, not a discovery
  experience.
- **Discord and Reddit communities:** good for meeting other gamers, but they have no
  personalised game recommendations and no way to compare tastes.

**What makes Game Discovery different:** it combines personal game discovery, a simple swipe-based
way to explore, recommendations that explain themselves, a personal game collection and social
discovery of gamers with a similar taste, all in one experience.
